Who we serve, and how

  • Private, charter and religious schools — the full menu, including daily route coverage, athletics, field trips and event transportation.
  • Public school districts — supplemental capacity: athletics and field trips, overflow and vendor-pool coverage, and alternative transportation including McKinney-Vento and special-education runs.
  • Emergency coverage — when a provider falls through, we work our carrier network to source replacement capacity. Emergency capacity is sourced, never guaranteed.
  • Right-sized vehicles — small student groups ride Type A buses and MFSAB activity vehicles, matched to the group and the run.
