Family Logistics

One Team. Every Stage Of The College Journey.

Families should not need five vendors to solve one college problem. We coordinate the rides, the moves and the storage — so parents stop being the project managers of college logistics.

The problem we solve

Every year, the same scramble: a ride home for Thanksgiving, a flight at winter break, a dorm to empty in May, boxes with nowhere to go for the summer. Each one means a different company, a different phone number, a different set of rules — and a parent holding all of it together from three states away.

Legacy Transportation Network replaces the scramble with one coordination team that knows your family, your student's school, and what comes next on the calendar.

What we are — and what we are not

Legacy Transportation Network is a logistics coordinator, a vendor network manager and your family's single point of contact for college transportation, moves and storage. We are not a bus company, a moving company or a storage facility — the driving, lifting and warehousing are performed by licensed, insured operating partners we select and manage. That structure is deliberate: it keeps one accountable team on your side of every transaction.

Students purchase convenience. Parents purchase peace of mind. We are built for both.
